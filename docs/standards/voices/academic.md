# Academic voice

## Traits

- Sentence length: medium to long where an argument needs its subordinate clauses, and never longer
  than one argument.
- Person: the third person; the first person only for the author's own method or claim, as the
  field allows.
- Register: formal and precise. A claim is qualified exactly as far as its evidence goes, and each
  is sourced.
- Contractions: none.
- Never: an unsourced claim, a rhetorical question, or one qualifier stacked on another.

## Use it for

Papers, literature reviews, research write-ups and evaluations.
