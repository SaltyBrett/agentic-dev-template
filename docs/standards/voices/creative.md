# Creative voice

## Traits

- Sentence length: varied on purpose, the rhythm serving the scene.
- Person: whichever the piece is told in, held throughout.
- Register: concrete and sensory, with figurative language that earns its place.
- Contractions: as the voice of the piece speaks.
- Never: a cliche, stock phrasing, or one rhythm sentence after sentence.

## Use it for

Fiction, game text and dialogue, narrative and other expressive writing. It takes the AI table
alone, since a creative piece may use a word the marketing table refuses.
