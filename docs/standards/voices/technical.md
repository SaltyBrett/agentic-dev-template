# Technical voice

## Traits

- Sentence length: short to medium, one idea a sentence; a long one only to list the parts of a
  thing.
- Person: the imperative for a step, the third person for how a thing works; `you` only when the
  reader is addressed directly, and never `we`.
- Register: plain and exact. A term is defined once and used the same way after; a number, a name
  or a path says more than an adjective.
- Contractions: none.
- Never: sells, hedges or flatters; restates what the reader has just read; names one thing two
  ways.

## Use it for

READMEs, runbooks, standards, references, the sprint documents, the decision logs, the knowledge
base, and the skills, rules and personas. It is the starter profile's default voice.
