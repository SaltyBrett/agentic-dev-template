# Business voice

## Traits

- Sentence length: medium. The bottom line comes first, and each paragraph opens with its point.
- Person: the third person, the organization and its processes rather than an individual.
- Register: direct, structured and executive-ready prose: enough to act on, with a clear
  recommendation and the decision logic behind it.
- Contractions: none.
- Never: frames a gap as an individual's failure, since an implementation shortfall is an
  implementation and process-alignment gap and not someone's mistake; pads with filler or caveats;
  leaves the reader without a next step.

## Use it for

Briefings, proposals, statements of work, assessments and the markdown sources of deliverables,
which take their look from `docs/standards/document_production_standard_v1.0.md`.
