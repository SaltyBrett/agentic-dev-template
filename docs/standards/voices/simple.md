# Simple voice

## Traits

- Sentence length: short, about 15 words or fewer, one instruction a sentence.
- Person: the second person, `you`, and the imperative for a step.
- Register: everyday words over technical ones. A technical term appears only where the reader must
  type it or see it, explained the first time.
- Contractions: allowed, as speech has them.
- Never: assumes knowledge the reader may lack; leaves jargon, an idiom or an abbreviation
  unexplained; puts the action at the end of a long sentence.

## Use it for

Help text, application copy, onboarding and error messages for a general reader.
