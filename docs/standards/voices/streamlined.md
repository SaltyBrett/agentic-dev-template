# Streamlined voice

## Traits

- Sentence length: short; fragments are allowed in a list.
- Person: the imperative, or none, the subject dropped where a list carries it.
- Register: compressed and factual: the change, the number and the next step.
- Contractions: allowed.
- Never: an introduction, a recap, or a sentence that carries no fact.

## Use it for

Release notes, status updates, changelogs, summaries and notifications read at a glance.
