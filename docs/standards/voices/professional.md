# Professional voice

## Traits

- Sentence length: medium and varied.
- Person: `we` for the team where it acts, `you` for the reader, with formal address.
- Register: courteous, measured and precise; formal without stiffness.
- Contractions: sparing, and none in a formal report.
- Never: slang, an exclamation mark, flattery of the reader, or a claim beyond the evidence.

## Use it for

Correspondence, reports and summaries for clients, partners and stakeholders outside the team.
